## 💾 Managing Backups Safely

Backups protect your work. If an app update or a change does not go as planned, a backup gives you something to fall back on.

A simple routine works well for most people. Create a backup before you update an app. Create a backup before you build from source. Create a backup before you make any change you are unsure about.

Inside craft-apps-manager, the backup area lets you create a backup and keep your backups organized. Store your backups somewhere you will remember, and give them names that make sense to you, such as the app name and the date.

Most problems have simple fixes. Try these steps in order.

**An app is not behaving correctly.** Create a backup if you have not already, then check for updates. Applying the newest version often resolves the issue.
